My Little Accountant Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. About This Policy

My Little Accountant helps users organize income, expenses, bills, savings goals, transactions, and account balances. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle:

  • Account information, such as your email address and account identifier.
  • Financial information you enter, including income, expenses, bills, amounts set aside for bills, transactions, and savings goals.
  • App preferences, including bill reminder settings.
  • Technical information processed by the app's service providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate users, synchronize supported data, calculate and display financial information, provide bill reminders, and maintain and protect the service.

4. Local Storage and Service Providers

Some app information may be stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Account information and financial records are also synchronized with online backend services.

My Little Accountant uses Supabase for authentication and backend data services and Netlify-hosted functions to support backend operations. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Financial Information

The app is designed for manual financial tracking. Connecting a bank account is not required for its manual-tracking features. You are responsible for the information you enter. My Little Accountant is a budgeting tool, not a bank or financial institution.

6. Notifications

If you enable bill reminders and grant notification permission, the app may schedule notifications about upcoming bill due dates. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings and reminder preferences through the app.

7. Data Security

The app uses Supabase authentication and backend services to support account access and data synchronization. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

The app does not currently provide an in-app option to delete your account and associated server-side data. To request account or data deletion, contact us at m.ereyes001@gmail.com.

We will review requests and explain any verification requirements or limitations that apply. Data retention and deletion timing may depend on the systems involved and applicable legal requirements.

9. Analytics and Advertising

We have not identified a dedicated analytics or advertising SDK in the app's currently reviewed package dependency list. However, the app's service providers may process technical logs for operation, security, and troubleshooting. This section should be updated if further review identifies additional analytics, diagnostics, advertising, or tracking.

10. Children's Privacy

My Little Accountant is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances that require action under applicable law, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features, providers, or data practices change. The latest version and update date will be available at this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or account and data deletion requests, contact m.ereyes001@gmail.com.
